About GILLE Grand Azure Hotel

A modern coastal hotel created for guests who value comfort, relaxation, and genuine hospitality.

OUR STORY

A Hotel Inspired by the Coast

GILLE Grand Azure Hotel was created with a simple idea: to give guests a comfortable place to stay while keeping them close to the beauty of the coast.

From our welcoming rooms to our relaxed dining spaces and ocean-facing amenities, every part of the hotel is designed to create a peaceful and enjoyable experience.

Whether you are travelling with family, spending time with someone special, or simply taking a break from everyday life, GILLE Hotel offers a place where you can slow down and enjoy your stay.
